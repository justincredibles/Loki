       s
      
    

    

      
◈ PROXIMITY RADAR

      
The person radar: nearby BLE devices plotted by signal range. Radius is real; angle is a fixed per-device slot.

      
    

    

      
◈ GPS TRACK

      
Record a 2-minute GPX track from the LoRa cap's GPS. Needs open sky for a fix.

      
    

    

      
◈ AUDIT WATCHDOG

      
Full audit, diffed against the last one: new devices, new ports, new CVEs. Also runs nightly at ~03:30 on a timer.

      
    

    

      
◈ PROBE LOGGER

      
Monitor-mode ears on the dongle: which devices are asking for which networks by name. The preferred-network list is a travel diary.

      
    

    

      
◈ TRACKER WATCH

      
Listen 90 s for AirTag / SmartTag / Tile beacons and grade them against the on-device history — persistence is the tell.

      
    

    

      
◈ SUB-GHZ SWEEP

      
Listen on 433 + 915 MHz bands (needs the CC1101 cap fitted).

      
    

    

      
◈ WPA-SEC

      
Upload captures to wpa-sec.stanev.org, download cracked passwords.

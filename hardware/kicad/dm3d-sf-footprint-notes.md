# Hirose DM3D-SF footprint — dimension notes

Footprint: `loki.pretty/Hirose_DM3D-SF.kicad_mod` (generated by the `Hirose DM3D-SF`
section at the end of `gen_footprints.py`).
Part: Hirose DM3D-SF microSD card connector, HRS No. 609-0025-8 (JLC C719027) — Loki SD1.

## Source

Hirose **DM3 Series datasheet/catalog**, DM3D section (connector drawing + "Recommended
PCB mounting pattern" for DM3D-SF), X-ON mirror of the Hirose catalog
(`hirose_dm3dsf_apr22_xonlink.pdf`). Every pad dimension below comes from that drawing —
no third-party footprint data was used.

### How the numbers were extracted (read this before trusting them)

The pattern is a dimensioned CAD drawing, so two independent measurements were made
and cross-checked:

1. **Vector pass** — pad outlines, centrelines, dimension lines/arrowheads were read
   from the PDF's vector data (page points).
2. **Raster pass** — the page was rendered at 700 dpi and the pad outline edges were
   re-measured from pixel runs (this recovered the four side pads, whose outlines sit
   in a PDF form object the vector pass does not enumerate).

Scale calibration: contact-pad centreline pitch in the drawing = 9.136 pt, labelled
**P = 1.1 mm** → **8.3052 pt/mm**. Cross-checks of labelled dimensions against the
measured drawing geometry all agree within 0.01 mm: overall span 7.7 (= 7 × 1.1),
body 11.95 × 11.45, contact length 1.75, switch-A position 10.7 / 9.15, switch-B
centreline 8.35, right shell-tab centreline 7.5. All footprint values are the
drawing values rounded to 0.01 mm.

Coordinates: origin = centre of the connector body outline as drawn in the pattern
(11.95 × 11.45 mm). +X = toward pin 1 end of the contact row, +Y = toward the
card-insertion (front) edge — i.e. the footprint matches the pattern drawing viewed
from above, rear/switch end at −Y. The pattern's own reference dimension "(4.5)"
(pin-8 centreline to body centre) reproduces as 4.52 mm in the footprint.

### Contact row dimensions (drawing labels)

- Pitch **1.1 mm**, span pin-8→pin-1 centrelines **7.7 mm** (labelled; footprint
  reproduces exactly 1.10 × 7 and 7.70).
- Pad length **1.75 mm** (labelled, vertical dimension at the right of the row).
- Pad width **0.70 mm** — from the drawn outline (0.698 mm measured). The "0.55"
  label near the row is *not* the pad width: its arrowheads span from the front-left
  shell tab's right edge to the pin-8 centreline (a position dimension, verified from
  the dimension-line geometry).
- Row position: pad top edge on the labelled contact-row reference line; row centre
  at Y = +5.35 mm, i.e. pads straddle the body front edge (Y = +5.725) with 0.50 mm
  of pad outside the body outline — exactly as drawn.

### Pad-numbering decisions

- **Contact numbering direction** — verified against the connector drawing's front
  elevation (the view under the top view, leaders labelled `8(DAT1) 7(DAT0) 6(VSS)
  5(CLK)` on the left, `4(VDD) 3(CMD) 2(CD/DAT3) 1(DAT2)` on the right). The front
  view's left↔right maps 1:1 onto the top view / mounting pattern, so in the pattern
  the row runs **pin 8 at −X … pin 1 at +X**. Functions match the task brief
  (#1 DAT2, #2 CD/DAT3, #3 CMD, #4 VDD, #5 CLK, #6 VSS, #7 DAT0, #8 DAT1).
- **Switch (A) = pad 9** — top-view leader "Card Detection Switch (A)" points to the
  small terminal protruding past the rear edge at the +X corner = the pattern pad
  dimensioned 1 wide, 9.15/10.7 position. ✔
- **Switch (B) = pad 10** — top-view leader "Card Detection Switch (B)" points to the
  terminal protruding past the left (−X) edge near the rear = the pattern pad whose
  centreline is labelled 8.35. Its left edge sits 0.40 mm outside the body outline,
  matching the pattern's "0.4MIN" label. ✔
- Switch behaviour per datasheet: **Normally Open — without card: open; card
  inserted: A–B closed.** (Loki §9 uses A → CD net / ESP32 IO7, B → GND.)
- Pad 10 (B) is an ordinary numbered pad, so the SD1 schematic symbol needs a real
  pin 10 (it cannot "inherit" GND from a same-numbered pad the way the MP tabs share
  "MP"); wire pin 10 to GND in the schematic.
- **MP ×4** — the datasheet states the metal cover has 4 PCB connection points;
  the four remaining pattern pads are exactly those shell solder tabs (front-left,
  front-right, left side, right side). All share the KiCad pad number "MP".

## Other drawing features carried into the footprint

- **Silkscreen**: body outline 11.95 × 11.45 mm; outline of a fully inserted card
  (11 mm wide, rear edge 4.35 mm proud of the front body edge, from the drawing's
  (15.8) card-fully-inserted dimension minus the 11.45 body depth); an insertion
  arrow pointing into the slot; pin-1 dot next to pad 1.
- **Courtyard**: (−6.65, −6.50) to (+6.65, +6.50) — pad extremes plus ~0.25 mm.
- **Note-3 "No conductive traces" zones** (hatched in the pattern) are drawn on
  **F.Fab for reference only** — keep copper/traces out of them during layout:
  - Zone 1 (rear edge, switch area): (−1.97, −5.73) to (+0.52, −3.73) — 2.49 × 2.00 mm.
  - Zone 2 (under the card slot): (−4.93, −1.52) to (+3.58, +0.48) — 8.51 × 2.00 mm;
    its Y bounds are the pattern's labelled 6MIN / 4MAX from the contact-row line.

## UNVERIFIED / flagged items

1. **Three pads have no explicit size labels in the pattern** — switch (B) (1.45 ×
   1.00), left shell tab width (0.80), front-left shell tab width (1.30). Their sizes
   come from the drawing's *drawn outlines*, measured two independent ways (vector +
   700-dpi raster) agreeing within ~0.02 mm, on the same calibrated scale that
   reproduces every labelled dimension to ≤0.01 mm. Low risk, but they are measured,
   not labelled.
2. **Shell-tab width asymmetry is as drawn**: front-right tab measures 1.49 mm wide
   vs front-left 1.30 mm (the left tab is squeezed between the body edge and pin 8 —
   only 0.20 mm clearance). Not a typo in this file; it matches the drawing.
3. Contact centres were nudged ≤0.006 mm when rounding to 0.01 mm so the row keeps
   the labelled uniform 1.10 mm pitch and 7.70 mm span exactly.
4. Nothing here was checked against a **physical** DM3D-SF (no sample on hand).
   Before fabrication, print the footprint 1:1 and test-fit a real connector —
   the same standing advice as the other Loki custom footprints.
5. The pattern does not dimension a stencil aperture separately; pads use the
   standard full-size paste aperture (same as the other Loki footprints).
